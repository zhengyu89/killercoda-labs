This lab tests:

1. Creating a ClusterIP Service (`port` and `targetPort`)
2. Exposing a Deployment as a NodePort Service
3. Finding a Service's `nodePort`
4. Reaching a Pod directly and through a Service
5. Creating a headless Service
6. **Advanced:** writing an EndpointSlice by hand
7. **Advanced:** reaching a Service in another namespace

Each task names its namespace. Click **Check** after each task.
